About Dataset:
	1-We present FracAtlas, a new dataset of X-Ray scans curated from the images collected from 3 major hospitals in Bangladesh. 
	2-Our dataset includes 4,083 images that have been manually annotated for bone fracture classification, localization, and segmentation with the help of 2 expert radiologists and an orthopedist using the open-source labeling platform, makesense.ai. 
	3-There are 717 images with 922 instances of fractures.

FracAtlas: 
	*A Dataset for Fracture Classification, Localization and Segmentation.
